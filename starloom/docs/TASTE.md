# Taste

These are the judgements that make a batch worth keeping. They are the point of the project;
the code exists to serve them.

**Restraint.** One world, one material, one star. A piece that tries two of anything looks generated.

**Contrast at the star.** The eye must land on the star within a second. Everything within 1.5 R of it
is quieter than the rest of the composition.

**Edges tell the material.** Torn edges wobble. Cut edges are straight. Printed edges are slightly off
register. Leaded edges are heavy and dark. Mixed edges in one piece read as a mistake.

**Flat colour, few colours.** Eight to twelve tokens. No gradients. Translucency only as a printed
overlap (multiply) or a deliberate wash, never as a softening device.

**Asymmetry with weight.** The star is off centre. The heaviest panel sits opposite it. Nothing is
centred except by accident, and the accident is rare.

**Motion is one gesture.** The star turns. Perhaps something inside it or around it turns the other way.
Nothing else moves. Backgrounds that animate compete with the star and cost the viewer the loop.

**Variation comes from placement, not from more stuff.** Twelve pieces differ in where the star sits,
which panels are large, and which motif leads. They do not differ by adding motifs until something
feels different.

**Reject freely.** A theme that yields one good piece in four is not finished. Render thirty-six, look at
the contact sheet, and change the theme until nine in twelve would hang on a wall.
